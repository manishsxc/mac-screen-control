## ✨ Features

- 🔄 **Two-Way Sharing:** Share your Mac screen with your Android tablet/phone, or share your Android screen with your Mac.
- 🔢 **Simple 6-Digit PIN:** Connect instantly on the same Wi-Fi using an easy 6-digit code. No login or accounts required.
- 🎯 **Flexible Sharing Options:** Choose to share your entire display or just a single specific application window.
- 🎮 **Full Remote Control:** Control your Mac from your tablet touch screen, or control your Android phone/tablet directly using your Mac mouse and keyboard.
- 💎 **Liquid Glass UI:** Modern translucent frosted glass design that automatically adapts to macOS and Android Light/Dark modes.
- 🚀 **Auto-Update Notifications:** Automatically alerts you when a new update is released so you always have the latest version.
- 🔒 **100% Private & Local:** All communication stays on your local Wi-Fi network. No cloud tracking, no subscriptions.

## 🚀 How It Works

### Option 1: Share from Mac to Android
1. Open **DisplayMac** on your Mac and click **Share Screen**.
2. Select whether you want to share your **Entire Screen** or a **Specific Window**.
3. A **6-digit PIN** will appear on your Mac.
4. On your Android device, open DisplayMac, tap **Receive / Control**, enter the PIN, and tap **Connect**.

### Option 2: Share from Android to Mac
1. Open **DisplayMac** on your Android device and tap **Share Screen**.
2. Note the generated **6-digit PIN**.
3. On your Mac, click **Receive / Control**, type the PIN, and connect.
4. You can now view and control your Android screen directly from your Mac!
