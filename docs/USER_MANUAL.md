## 1. Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- Mac running macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or newer.
- Android phone or tablet running Android 8.0 or newer.
- Both devices connected to the **same local Wi-Fi network** (or connected via USB-C cable).

---

## 2. How to Share Your Mac Screen with Android

1. Open **DisplayMac** on your Mac.
2. Under **Share My Screen**, choose whether to share your **Entire Screen** or a **Specific Application Window**.
3. Click **Create Session & Share**. A **6-digit PIN** will be displayed (e.g. `583921`).
4. On your Android tablet or phone:
   - Open DisplayMac.
   - Switch to **Receive / Control**.
   - Type the 6-digit PIN and tap **Connect**.
5. Your Mac screen will appear on your Android device. You can touch, drag, and use a stylus to control the Mac.

---

## 3. How to Share Your Android Screen with Mac

1. Open **DisplayMac** on your Android device.
2. Tap **Share Screen**. When prompted by Android, grant permission and choose **Entire Screen** or **Single App**.
3. A **6-digit PIN** will be generated on Android.
4. On your Mac:
   - Switch to **Receive / Control Peer**.
   - Type the 6-digit PIN and click **Receive & Control**.
5. Your Android screen will now be visible in a window on your Mac, and you can control it with your mouse and keyboard.

## 4. Submitting Feedback

Inside the app:
- Click or tap the **Feedback & Support** button.
- Type your feedback or suggestions.
- The app will automatically send or open your mail app directed to **sumitranjanhisu@gmail.com**.

## 5. Updates

Whenever a new version is pushed to GitHub, DisplayMac will check for the update on startup and notify you with a direct download button to install the latest release automatically.
